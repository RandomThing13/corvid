Inspect the source

Download one of these public development PDFs, then upload it in Stoa. These examples use native PDF text or calibrated geometry without model calls.

  • Printed room areas: ask “What is the combined area of room 2.01 and room 2.02?” Expected printed sum: 27.66 m².
  • Unprinted room geometry: ask “What is the area of room 4.23?” Inspect the room polygon and scale bar.

A missing room such as 99.99 must be identified as incomplete, not accepted as a complete total.
